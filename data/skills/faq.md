## How do I join the club?

Membership is through the UWA Student Guild club page, not through Discord — see
`recall_club_links` for the link. Paying Guild membership is what makes you a member for
constitutional purposes (voting at general meetings, standing for committee).

Being in the Discord server is free and open; it does not make you a member.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. The club is for anyone interested in AI, whatever their degree or skill level — including people
who have never written a line of code and people from outside CS entirely. If someone is worried
about being underqualified, reassure them plainly.

## Can I use AI tools on my assignments?

Rules vary by unit, and the unit outline is authoritative. Point people at the UWA Library's AI
guide (see `recall_club_links`) and tell them to check their unit outline or ask the unit
coordinator. Help members understand and learn the material; don't produce submittable work for
them.
