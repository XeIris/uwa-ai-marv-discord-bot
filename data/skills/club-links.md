# UWA AI Club — official links and study resources

All links below are public. Share them freely. Never invent a URL that isn't on this
page — if someone asks for something not listed, say you don't have an official link
for it and point them at the committee (`list_committee`).

## Club channels (official)

- **UWA Student Guild club page** — official club listing, membership sign-up:
  https://www.uwastudentguild.com/clubs/uwa-artificial-intelligence-club
- **LinkedIn** — announcements, sponsors, industry-facing posts:
  https://au.linkedin.com/company/uwa-ai-club
- **Instagram** — event promos, photos, day-to-day updates:
  https://www.instagram.com/uwaaiclub/
- **Discord permanent invite** — this server, safe to reshare anywhere:
  https://discord.gg/8sQXmT9qRg

Joining the club (paying membership) happens through the Guild club page, not
through Discord. Discord is free to be in; Guild membership is what makes someone a
member for constitutional purposes (see `recall_constitution`).

## UWA resources (not run by the club)

Make clear these are the University's own pages, not ours, and that course
requirements and unit availability change — tell people to confirm on the UWA page
or with their faculty rather than trusting a summary.

- **AI study/courses at UWA** — degrees, majors and units in AI:
  https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/courses/artificial-intelligence
- **UWA Library AI subject guide** — databases, reading, research help, and the
  library's guidance on using AI tools in coursework:
  https://guides.library.uwa.edu.au/artificial_intelligence/

## Academic-integrity guardrail

If someone asks for help with assessed work, help them learn — explain concepts,
debug their reasoning, point at the library guide above — and don't write
submittable answers for them. UWA's rules on AI use vary per unit; tell them to
check their unit outline.
