# UWA academic calendar

**Source:** <https://www.uwa.edu.au/students/your-studies/important-dates> — last checked 2026-08-08.

These dates carry money and academic-record consequences. Quote them, always link the page above,
and tell the user to confirm there. Never estimate or extrapolate a date that isn't written below —
if you're asked about a year this sheet doesn't cover, say you don't have it.

All dates are Perth time, standard teaching periods only.

## What the deadlines actually mean

- **Early withdrawal** — drop by this date and the unit does not appear on your academic record at
  all, and you aren't charged.
- **Census date** — the money deadline. Withdraw on or before it and you don't pay for the unit;
  withdraw after it and you are liable for the fees (and it uses up Commonwealth support /
  HELP entitlement) even though you get no mark.
- **Academic withdrawal** — the record deadline. Withdraw after it and you get a fail-equivalent
  notation on your transcript.

If someone is asking about dropping a unit near either deadline, give them the date, make clear
which consequence applies, and tell them to talk to UWA Student Advising rather than relying on a
Discord bot — this affects their fees and transcript.

## Caveats to state when you answer

- **Exam periods move**, and a personal exam timetable is published on studentConnect about five
  weeks beforehand. Never tell someone when *their* exam is; tell them where to look.
- **Non-standard teaching periods** (summer/winter units, some postgraduate and online units, and
  most non-semester offerings) have completely different dates that are **not** in this sheet:
  <https://www.uwa.edu.au/students/your-studies/non-standard-teaching-dates>.
- UWA publishes teaching *weeks* slightly differently in places (the askUWA teaching-week table and
  the important-dates page don't always end on the same day). If precision matters to the user —
  and near a census date it does — send them to the official page rather than arbitrating it.
