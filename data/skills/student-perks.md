# Free and discounted tools for students

**These offers change constantly** — providers cut credits, pause sign-ups, and leave programmes
with little notice. Every entry below carries a *last checked* date. When you answer:

1. give the entry as written,
2. say when it was last checked,
3. tell the user to confirm on the provider's own page before relying on it.

Never invent an offer, a credit amount, or an eligibility rule that isn't written here. If someone
asks about a tool that isn't listed, say it isn't on the club's list rather than guessing.

**Maintainers:** review this file at the start of each semester. The churn is real — see the
GitHub/DigitalOcean entries below for what a few months did.

## GitHub Student Developer Pack

*Last checked 2026-08-08.* <https://education.github.com/pack>

Free for verified students: GitHub Pro, plus a bundle of third-party offers (domains, CI, dev
tools). Verification needs proof of enrolment — a UWA email or student ID.

Two changes worth knowing, because both bit people recently:

- **GitHub Copilot.** From 12 March 2026 verified students moved to a Copilot Student plan with
  200 monthly AI credits and unlimited code completions. **New sign-ups for the free student plan
  were paused in April 2026**, citing compute demand — students verified before the pause kept
  theirs; new pack members get Copilot Free until sign-ups reopen. Tell people to check current
  status rather than promising them free Copilot.
- **DigitalOcean has left the pack.** Its student credit ended and all GitHub Student Pack
  promotional credits there **expired 31 July 2026**. Do not offer this one.

## JetBrains student licence

*Last checked 2026-08-08.* <https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/>

Free full licence for all JetBrains IDEs (PyCharm, IntelliJ, CLion, DataGrip…) for students,
renewable annually while enrolled. Verify with a university email or ISIC. Useful for anyone doing
CITS units in Java or Python who wants more than a text editor.

## Free tiers worth knowing for AI/ML coursework

*Last checked 2026-08-08.* No sign-up perk needed — these are free to anyone, students included.

- **Google Colab** — free GPU/TPU notebook sessions with usage caps and no guaranteed
  availability; sessions are ephemeral, so save to Drive or Git.
  <https://colab.research.google.com>
- **Kaggle Notebooks** — free weekly GPU/TPU quota, plus datasets and competitions.
  <https://www.kaggle.com/code>

Both are the realistic answer to "how do I train something without a GPU". Warn people that free
sessions get reclaimed and to checkpoint their work.
